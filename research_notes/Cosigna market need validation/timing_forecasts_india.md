# Timing of demand for money-moving agent controls (now vs 2-3 years), and India specifics (as of 24 Oct 2026)

Note on sourcing: 15 tool calls. Gartner's own site returned HTTP 403, so some Gartner figures rely on press coverage of Gartner releases. Where only secondary or vendor sources exist, that is flagged.

## Analyst forecasts on enterprise agentic AI adoption and failure

### Takeaway
Analysts expect many agent projects to fail in the near term, and they name weak risk controls as one of the causes. They also forecast that autonomous decision-making will grow and that a "guardian agent" control layer will become a meaningful share of spend around 2028-2030. Adoption today is wide but shallow: about a quarter of firms are scaling agents anywhere, and no more than 10% are scaling them in any single function.

### Cited Findings
- Gartner (June 2025): more than 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by the end of 2027, because of spiralling costs, unclear business value or inadequate risk controls. Analyst Anushree Verma said current models lack the maturity to autonomously achieve complex goals or follow nuanced instructions over time. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/artificial-intelligence/over-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-scrapped-by-2027-says-gartner); [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3316025/over-40-agentic-ai-projects-forecast-be-scrapped-2027-due-lack-value); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/gartner-agentic-ai-projects-cancelled-2027)
- Same Gartner release: only about 130 of the "thousands" of agentic AI vendors are real; Gartner calls the rest "agent washing". It also forecasts that at least 15% of day-to-day work decisions will be made autonomously by agentic AI by 2028, up from 0% in 2024. — [CRN India](https://www.crn.in/?p=72052); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/artificial-intelligence/over-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-scrapped-by-2027-says-gartner)
  - Conflict: Fortune's August 2025 coverage gives the 15% figure, and the figure for one-third of enterprise apps embedding agentic AI, as "by 2030". Gartner's earlier releases used 2028. Treat the year as 2028 per the Gartner-sourced coverage, but check it against the original. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/08/29/gartner-predicts-ai-fueled-lonely-enterprise-finance-workers-cfo-dont-take-action)
- Gartner (11 June 2025, primary press release): "guardian agents" will capture 10-15% of the agentic AI market by 2030. Gartner defines them as AI systems that review or monitor other agents, or block and redirect their actions. The release cites a May 2025 Gartner webinar poll of 147 CIOs and IT leaders: 24% had deployed fewer than a dozen agents and 4% more than a dozen. — [Gartner press release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-11-gartner-predicts-that-guardian-agents-will-capture-10-15-percent-of-the-agentic-ai-market-by-2030); [Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/cloud-security/agentic-ai-gartner-srm-summit)
- Gartner (IT Symposium/Xpo, Oct/Nov 2025): AI agents will intermediate more than $15 trillion of B2B spend by 2028, and about 90% of B2B buying will be agent-intermediated. Most coverage comes from vendors with an interest (Two, Abmatic, Norce), and the month varies by source. — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/11/28/gartner-ai-agents-15-trillion-in-b2b-purchases-by-2028/); [Two](https://www.two.inc/resources/two-cents-blog/ai-agents-will-run-90-of-b2b-buying-by-2028-what-gartners-15-trillion-forecast-means-for-payments-and-credit)
- Gartner finance-specific (2026 Finance Technology Bullseye; survey of 314 organisations, Sep-Oct 2025): investment intent in AI agents is high, tied to bets on "autonomous finance", but current value and adoption are only moderate and most deployments are still pilots. Gartner warns that hype and fragmentation will waste budgets. AI could add about 10 margin points by 2029. — [CFO Dive](https://www.cfodive.com/news/ai-unlock-10-margin-points-growth-cfos-2029-gartner/818877/)
- Reported Gartner figures from Finance Symposium 2026: by 2029, 30% of finance teams will have maximised AI-driven efficiency in transactional processes. The 59% figure for finance functions using AI in 2025 comes from secondary sources only. — [OneAdvanced](https://www.oneadvanced.com/resources/five-standout-themes-from-gartner-finance-symposium-2026/)
- McKinsey, State of AI 2025 (Nov 2025): 23% of respondents' organisations are scaling an agentic AI system somewhere and another 39% are experimenting. The 62% total is derived, not a McKinsey headline. In any given function, no more than 10% are scaling agents. Use is most common in IT and knowledge management. Overall AI use is 88%, up from 78%. — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-2025)

### Inferences
- Gartner's cancellation forecast lists "inadequate risk controls" as a failure cause, and its guardian-agent forecast targets 2030. Together they suggest the control layer is a 2027-2030 budget line, with early buyers in 2026.
- Finance and treasury are not where agent use is most common today (IT and knowledge management are), so money-moving agents are probably 12-24 months behind the general agent curve in enterprises.
- Methodology is thin: a 147-person webinar poll and vendor-repeated B2B figures. Cite these as directional, not as market sizing.

### Gaps
- No Forrester or IDC primary figures were retrieved on agent adoption or failure. None were searched successfully, so leave them out unless verified.
- Gartner's 25 June 2025 release could not be fetched directly (HTTP 403), so the survey sample size behind the 40% figure is unverified.
- I found no analyst forecast specific to AP, treasury or payout agents, or to "agent spend controls" as a category.

## Agentic commerce and agent-initiated payment volume forecasts (consumer vs B2B finance)

### Takeaway
Forecasts for consumer agentic shopping in 2030 range from about $144B to $5T, depending on definition. Measured volume today is under 1% of e-commerce. Forecasts for B2B and finance operations (e.g. Gartner's $15T intermediated B2B spend) are larger but looser, and none of them measure agent-executed payments specifically.

### Cited Findings
- McKinsey (Oct 2025): agentic commerce could orchestrate up to $1T of US retail revenue by 2030, and $3-5T globally. — [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2025/10/20/mckinsey-forecast-5-trillion-agentic-commerce-sales-2030/)
- Morgan Stanley (Dec 2025): base case $190B and bull case $385B of US e-commerce by 2030 (10-20%), using a strict "meaningful autonomous action" definition. Bain: $300-500B by 2030 (15-25% of e-commerce, including agent-influenced purchases). eMarketer: about $144B by 2029 (only in-platform completed sales). Juniper: $1.5T in 2030, from $8B in 2026. All of these come via secondary or vendor summaries, not the original reports. — [Stellagent summary](https://stellagent.ai/insights/agentic-commerce-market-size-forecast-2030); [Tenten](https://tenten.co/shopify/agentic-commerce-market-size/)
- A Bernstein estimate puts agent-mediated transactions at less than 1% of e-commerce today (reported Sep 2026, secondary). — [Stellagent digest 14 Sep 2026](https://stellagent.ai/insights/ec-ai-news-digest-2026-09-14)
- Network milestones: Mastercard reported Europe's first live Agent Pay transaction with Santander on 2 March 2026, plus a live agentic transaction in Singapore with DBS and UOB. In India, Mastercard showed a "first fully authenticated agentic commerce payment" at the India AI Impact Summit 2026 in a controlled environment. Visa ran live agent-payment demos with 10 Indian banks and payment providers at GFF 2026. These are demos and pilots, not general availability. — [PaymentsJournal](https://www.paymentsjournal.com/mastercard-advances-agentic-ai-commerce-in-india/); [ai2.work](https://ai2.work/blog/santander-and-mastercard-complete-europe-s-first-live-ai-agent-payment); [Stellagent digest](https://stellagent.ai/insights/ec-ai-news-digest-2026-09-14)
- Ant International, Mastercard and Visa are working on a "Know-Your-Agent" interoperability framework (Sep 2026). — [TNGlobal](https://technode.global/2026/09/12/india-npci-ai-agent-registry-upi-payments/)

### Inferences
- Consumer agentic shopping is being built by the networks themselves (Agent Pay, Visa Intelligent Commerce, KYA), so a startup selling controls into that segment competes with the networks' own tooling.
- The business-finance side (payouts, AP, vendor payments, refunds) has no network-led control layer and is closer to where Cosigna sits. Its volume forecasts are weaker and less specific, which makes it a riskier sizing story but leaves less incumbent tooling.
- Forecast spread (roughly 35x between eMarketer and McKinsey) means any TAM claim should state its definition.

### Gaps
- I found no forecast of agent-initiated B2B payment volume (as opposed to "intermediated spend").
- The Morgan Stanley and Bain primary reports were not accessed.

## Leading indicators that the need is now (regulation, standards, budgets)

### Takeaway
The EU's high-risk AI obligations have slipped to December 2027 and August 2028, so EU regulation is a 2027-28 driver, not a 2026 one. The nearer drivers are payment-rule changes (India's authentication directions, effective 1 April 2026) and networks' agent-identity frameworks.

### Cited Findings
- EU AI Act Digital Omnibus: a provisional agreement was reached on 7 May 2026, deferring stand-alone Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 December 2027 and Annex I (product-embedded) obligations to 2 August 2028. The European Parliament voted to adopt it on 16 June 2026; Council approval and publication in the Official Journal were pending in June. Article 50 transparency duties largely stay on schedule, though sources differ on whether watermarking moves to 2 Dec 2026. — [Sidley Data Matters, 22 Jun 2026](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2026/06/22/eu-lawmakers-reach-provisional-agreement-to-delay-key-eu-ai-act-obligations/); [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines/)
- Note: credit scoring of natural persons is an Annex III high-risk use, but generic payment-execution agents are not obviously high-risk under the Act. (Inference from the Annex III scope, not verified in this session.)
- Gartner frames the guardian and control layer as a budget category, with 10-15% of agentic AI spend by 2030. — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-11-gartner-predicts-that-guardian-agents-will-capture-10-15-percent-of-the-agentic-ai-market-by-2030)

### Inferences
- In 2026 the "why now" argument is carried more by payment-system rules, network agent-identity schemes and incident risk than by AI-specific law. AI-law-driven audit demand (EU) mostly arrives in 2027-28, inside the 2-3 year window.

### Gaps
- Not researched this session (no sources retrieved): the status of US state laws (e.g. whether the Colorado AI Act's effective date was moved again), SEC or FINRA guidance on AI agents (FINRA's annual oversight reports may cover agents, unverified), NIST AI RMF agent profiles or overlays, and ISO/IEC 42001 certification uptake. Verify these separately before citing.

## India: RBI/NPCI moves, authentication and e-mandate rules, companies shipping, enterprise use

### Takeaway
India is building rails for delegated agent payments (Reserve Pay, UPI Circle, a planned Unified Agent Protocol and agent registry), but as of late October 2026 there is no NPCI technical specification, go-live date or liability framework. The only live items are pilots and betas.

NPCI's chairman has publicly said that AI may recommend, but authentication and settlement must follow "deterministic auditable rules". That stance lines up directly with Cosigna's design (exact-transaction approval and tamper-evident evidence).

### Cited Findings
Regulators and NPCI:
- RBI FREE-AI committee report, released 13 August 2025. The committee was set up on 26 December 2024 and produced 7 "Sutras" and 26 recommendations across 6 pillars, including board-approved AI policies for regulated entities. The recommendations are not binding. — [RBI press release](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=61018); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/banking/rbi-panel-proposes-ethical-ai-framework-for-financial-sector); non-binding status per [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-npci-ai-agents-upi-payments/)
- RBI Authentication Mechanisms for Digital Payment Transactions Directions, 2025: issued 25 September 2025, compliance due 1 April 2026. Two-factor authentication is the baseline. The Directions are technology-neutral and allow factors beyond SMS OTP, and issuers may add risk-based checks. Business Today (secondary) reports that at least one factor must be dynamic, i.e. unique to each transaction. Card issuers must validate AFA on non-recurring cross-border card-not-present transactions when the overseas merchant or acquirer requests it. — [RBI press release](https://website.rbi.org.in/web/rbi/-/press-releases/rbi-issues-directions-on-framework-on-authentication-mechanisms-for-digital-payment-transactions); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/news/story/rbi-issues-new-guidelines-on-digital-payment-authentication-effective-april-2026-495758-2025-09-25)
- E-mandates: MediaNama says the "RBI E-mandate Framework 2026" requires 24-hour pre-notification for each debit. A consumer advocate argued an agent protocol would either inherit this, making agent use cases impractical, or sit outside any RBI instrument until RBI writes a new one. — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-npci-ai-agents-upi-payments/)
- Delegation limits: Reserve Pay is reported as capped at Rs 10,000 for up to 90 days, and full UPI Circle delegation at Rs 15,000/month (bank caps). These come from secondary press, not NPCI circulars. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/npci-to-launch-agentic-payments-on-upi-report/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/india-plans-ai-powered-upi-payments-framework-through-unified-agent-protocol)
- Unified Agent Protocol (UAP): Business Standard first reported it on 9 July 2026, citing four unnamed people. Reuters reported on 1 September 2026, citing three people, that it would let agents pay on UPI without per-transaction approval, within pre-set limits, using UPI Circle and Reserve Pay. Reuters reported around 10 September 2026 that NPCI is building an agent registry to verify and monitor agents: it would start with UPI, could extend to cards and bill payments, and first use cases would be small, frequent payments such as groceries. NPCI declined to comment and has published no specification. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/npci-to-launch-agentic-payments-on-upi-report/); [TNGlobal](https://technode.global/2026/09/12/india-npci-ai-agent-registry-upi-payments/); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/india-prepares-agentic-payments-rollout-on-upi-network)
- GFF 2026 (Mumbai, 10 September 2026): NPCI chairman Ajay Kumar Choudhary said NPCI is "examining the protocols that may be required to identify and authorise digital agents within UPI". He also said: "Decision making and execution must remain separate," "AI may recommend, but authentication and final settlement must follow deterministic auditable rules," and interoperability must be "non-negotiable" with no "new walled gardens". He gave no launch date, no limits and nothing on liability. As of 29 September 2026, NPCI had not answered MediaNama on limits, liability or RBI approval. — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-npci-ai-agents-upi-payments/)
  - Conflict: some outlets say UAP was "unveiled" at GFF 2026, and one says NPCI launched AiNxt and AtOM to help partners build and certify agent workflows. MediaNama's account of the official keynote says only "examining". Treat UAP as announced intent, not a launched product. — [StartupFeed](https://startupfeed.in/gff-2026-agentic-ai-fintech-product-launches/); [CareerIndia](https://www.careerindia.com/news/ai-agent-upi-payments-npci-framework-fintech-hiring-trends-2026-011-65443.html)
- Dvara Research (Manvi Khanna) flagged liability for an agent that acts within a valid mandate but against the user's interest as an open question, citing FREE-AI Recommendation 8. A consumer advocate noted that UPI's dispute framework has no category for an agent that mis-executes a mandate. — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-npci-ai-agents-upi-payments/)

Companies (pilot, beta or launched, kept separate):
- PILOT: Razorpay + NPCI + OpenAI "Agentic Payments" in ChatGPT, announced 9 October 2025 at GFF 2025. It uses UPI Reserve Pay and UPI Circle, with Axis Bank and Airtel Payments Bank as banking partners and BigBasket (plus Vi per TechCrunch) as merchants. I found no evidence of general availability. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/tech-today/news/story/razorpay-npci-openai-launch-pilot-for-agentic-payments-where-ai-powered-shopping-meets-upi-497545-2025-10-09); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/india-pilots-ai-chatbot-led-e-commerce-with-chatgpt-gemini-claude-in-the-mix/)

Agents cover dispute response, failed-subscription retries, abandoned carts and cash-flow forecasting. The no-code builder is in beta and the blog still shows "early access". — [Razorpay newsroom](https://razorpay.com/newsroom/razorpay-launches-the-worlds-first-ai-native-agent-studio-for-payments-at-ftx26-powered-by-anthropics-claude/); [Razorpay blog](https://razorpay.com/blog/agent-studio-ai-agents-by-razorpay)
- BETA (most relevant to Cosigna): RazorpayX "bank with AI agents", 1 June 2026. A Payout Agent prepares payouts from a natural-language request and routes them to an OTP approval page. - Enterprise AP in India: PwC India has an agentic Intelligent Spend Management suite (Feb 2025) and CashFlo an AI payments and AP suite. Other sources are vendor "best practice" blogs. — [PwC/IDC](https://pwc.com/gx/en/about/analyst-relations/2025/idc-spotlight-agenic-ai-based-intelligent-spend-management-suite.html); [CXOToday](https://cxotoday.com/press-release/cashflo-expands-product-portfolio-with-ai-powered-payments-and-ap-automation-suite/)

### Inferences
- India's regulatory direction (two-factor authentication with a dynamic factor, 24-hour e-mandate pre-notification, NPCI's "deterministic auditable rules") favours a design where agents propose and humans or deterministic policy approve. That matches Cosigna's exact-transaction approval and evidence model. The flip side: if UAP lets agents pay "without per-transaction approval" inside NPCI-set limits, NPCI and the banks may themselves supply basic limits for consumer UPI.
- Cosigna's clearer opening in India is business payouts and AP. RazorpayX's Payout Agent relies on a single OTP, with no published per-agent limits or maker-checker. Each payment-gateway vendor controls only its own rail, so a cross-rail policy and evidence layer is not provided by any one of them. This is unverified with buyers.
- Timing for India: consumer agentic UPI depends on NPCI specification, RBI sign-off and liability rules, none of which existed as of October 2026, so likely 2027 or later. B2B payout and AP agents are in beta now (2026), so early adopters exist today.

### Gaps
- No NPCI circular or specification for UAP or the agent registry was found. Reserve Pay and UPI Circle limits are from press reports, not circulars.
- No primary source was found for the "RBI E-mandate Framework 2026" or its 24-hour rule; it is cited only via MediaNama.
- Nothing found on PhonePe or Juspay agentic payout launches, or on Swiggy/Zomato MCP payment integrations, beyond Cashfree's mention of Swiggy in its MCP Apps work.
- No survey quantifies Indian enterprise use of agents for payouts or AP.
- No status update found for the ChatGPT-UPI pilot after October 2025.
