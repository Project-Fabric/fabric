FAQ

Your questions, answered

Does Cosigna hold our money?

No. Money stays with your payment provider. Cosigna holds the provider key so your agents don’t, and decides which requests it uses it for.

Do we need to change our agents?

They call Cosigna’s tools (MCP or HTTP) instead of the payment API. The rest of their work stays the same.

Which payment systems work today?

Stripe in test mode, end to end, and a local ledger for trials. More rails are on the roadmap; we’ll say so when each is tested.

What happens if Cosigna is down?

Agents can’t move money through it, because they never had the key. Requests fail closed rather than paying unchecked.

What does the evidence prove?

That records weren’t changed after they were written, and that each decision follows from the rules in force. It proves what its verifier checks, nothing more.
